Scope and calculation

This independent portfolio case models a fictional power-unit demonstrator work package. Its purpose is to practise programme-management decisions at the interface of engineering, supply chain and finance.

  • Finish-to-start dependencies, zero-duration review milestones, frozen actuals and owner-entered remaining durations.
  • Single-capacity Dyno A and HIL rig. Resource-order arcs enter the backward pass for float and criticality.
  • One five-day calendar. No public-holiday exclusions in the demo. No overtime, FIA test allowances or full personnel capacity model.
  • Bottom-up cost forecast reconciles to actuals, remaining estimates, incremental recovery and late support.
  • Physical progress is distinct from acceptance. A mandatory record needs completed work, accepted state, reference, approver and valid date.

Uncertainty and limitations

A shared future supplier event affects both electrical deliveries, preserving their common exposure. Retest risk affects integrated endurance validation. Recovery probabilities are illustrative assumptions that require specialist validation.

Cost uncertainty includes late support only. It excludes uncertain material prices and retest charges. The sampling margin describes Monte Carlo sampling error, not real-world model accuracy.

Quantiles use the nearest-rank method. Work durations use nearest-day rounding with a one-day minimum for nonzero work. No predictions about Audi's delivery performance are made.

Reproduce and review

Run the Python CLI against the versioned CSV and JSON files. The report embeds its analysis and opens offline, with no external scripts or trackers.

CSV exports hold the activity-level results for each option. The Excel workbook provides a live deterministic planning view with a fixed resource order. Monte Carlo results must be regenerated in Python.

Input SHA-256 fingerprints and the random seed are included in the analysis JSON. Tests cover scheduling, capacity conflicts, preserved actuals, cost aggregation and missing release evidence.
